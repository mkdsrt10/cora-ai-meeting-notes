Part of `docs/specs/plugin-harness-local-proof.md`. Read it, the ADRs it lists and `GLOSSARY.md` first.

## What to build
Selection on the Recording page and To-dos → 'Send to Slack' → composer in the confirm panel. To: your channels, DM to yourself, DM to a Person with a confirmed Account (fetched on open, names only); default last destination per folder. Template message by default; Text model only with Slack Usage instructions. One `message.post`; posts as the user; permalink stored as a Link; no updates after posting.
